# Product Vision

## What we sell

Optimus Rufus is a SaaS that rewrites Amazon product listings so they are understood — and recommended — by Amazon's two new ranking systems: the [COSMO](../glossary.md) knowledge graph and the [Rufus](../glossary.md) conversational assistant. The product is sold as a fixed-scope engagement, not a tool subscription: a seller hands us an ASIN, we return an optimized listing plus a report explaining every change. The recurring SaaS layer is the dashboard that tracks rankings over time and re-runs the analysis as Rufus behavior shifts.

## Who buys

Amazon private-label sellers in health, supplements, and beauty. Mid-market: $1M–$20M annual GMV, already running PPC, already burned by generic SEO agencies. The ICP is firmographic (US marketplace, branded listing, not a wholesaler) plus intent (responded to outbound, booked a call). Buyers are operators, not marketers; they measure ROI in units moved, not impressions.

## Why now

Amazon is migrating ranking from keyword matching (A9) to intent understanding (COSMO) and from search to conversation (Rufus). Listings written for A9 — stuffed with keywords, no Q&A scaffolding, no Q/A pairs — are losing share of voice to listings that are structured to be *cited* by an answer engine. The 12–18 month window for early-mover advantage is open; once Rufus has learned which listings to cite, the moat is data, not copy.

## The optimization target

Two systems, one job.

- **COSMO** is Amazon's Common Sense Knowledge Graph. It encodes relationships between products, attributes, and shopper intents. A listing is well-aligned when its title, bullets, and A+ content map cleanly onto the attributes and intents COSMO expects for its category.
- **Rufus** is Amazon's conversational shopping assistant. It answers questions like "what is the best magnesium for sleep that doesn't upset my stomach" by retrieving and citing listings. A listing is well-aligned when its content is *retrievable* for the questions Rufus is being asked.

The product treats both as a single optimization target: structure content for COSMO, write Q&A scaffolding for Rufus.

## The 24-dimension semantic gap analysis

Every analyzed listing is scored on 24 dimensions spanning content (title, bullets, A+, description), structure (attribute coverage, Q/A pair count, comparison blocks), and discoverability (keyword alignment, intent alignment, competitor parity). The `SemanticGap` record carries one dimension at a time, with current score, target score, and priority [`ref: api/pipeline/pipeline.types.ts:42`](../../api/pipeline/pipeline.types.ts).

The dimension list is the product's secret menu — it is what we score, what we recommend against, and what we benchmark against competitors. It is not exposed to buyers as 24 numbers; it is presented as three composite scores plus a prioritized issue list.

## The Rufus Compatibility Score

A 0–100 composite that measures how well a listing is structured to be cited by Rufus. It rolls up Q/A pair coverage, question phrasing alignment, citation-friendly formatting, and answer completeness. The score is stored on `listing_analyses.rufusScore` [`ref: api/db/schema.ts:61`](../../api/db/schema.ts) and is the headline number the buyer sees.

`cosmoScore`, `semanticScore`, `contentScore`, and `visualScore` are stored alongside it [`ref: api/db/schema.ts:62`](../../api/db/schema.ts), giving the buyer a 4-axis scorecard. The AEO framing positions Rufus Score as the optimization north star; the other three scores are diagnostic.

## AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

The framing we sell under. [AEO](../glossary.md) is content structured to be cited by AI answer surfaces — Rufus on Amazon, ChatGPT for shopping queries, Perplexity for product research. We are not selling "SEO for Amazon"; we are selling "rank in the answer, not the result list." This framing buys us two things: a higher-margin positioning versus commodity listing writers, and a future-proof moat as more shopping traffic moves from search to conversation.

## The data we generate

The product's job is to fill the rows in three tables:

| Table | What fills it | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `prospects` | Leads, status, package type | Sales funnel [`ref: api/db/schema.ts:12`](../../api/db/schema.ts) |
| `listings` | Raw scraped Amazon listing data | `fetch` + `preprocess` stages [`ref: api/pipeline/definitions.ts:4`](../../api/pipeline/definitions.ts) |
| `listing_analyses` | The scorecard + AI-generated copy | `semantic` + `optimize` stages [`ref: api/db/schema.ts:52`](../../api/db/schema.ts) |

The `listing_analyses` table is the product's IP. Every column of copy (headline variants, Q/A pairs, audit narrative, PPC keywords, COSMO bundling suggestions) is generated by the pipeline and stored for later reuse, A/B testing, and the recurring SaaS re-runs.
